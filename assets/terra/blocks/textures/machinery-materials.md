# Machinery Materials

Created with the built-in image generation tool for the Phase 154 machinery
reference. These textures are only assigned by the kinetic/logistics asset
generators; existing furniture and tools keep their own materials.

- `machinery-oak-hd.png`: Seamless matte worked oak albedo, subtle horizontal
  grain and small knots, average warm mid-brown RGB near 155/105/48. No plank
  borders: fitted boards and gaps are actual model geometry.
- `machinery-iron-hd.png`: Seamless cool gray forged iron albedo near RGB
  120/128/135, fine brushed scratches and restrained shallow hammer marks.
  Explicitly no stones, paver pattern, baked bolts or lighting gradients.
- `machinery-belt-hd.png`: Seamless charcoal conveyor tread albedo near RGB
  95/100/106. Eight equal horizontal bands with thin recessed seams, no vertical
  grid or baked hardware; the ribbon material scrolls in the V direction.

All prompts requested flat orthographic material-only images, uniform lighting,
no labels or perspective, and restrained handcrafted voxel-game detail.
